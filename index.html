VIGIL AI

RTL verification impact & coverage-gap framework

An agentic AI framework that compares RTL versions, identifies modified modules, analyses downstream impact and prioritises the relevant test cases. It detects coverage gaps and corner cases (boundary conditions, FSM transitions, interface issues), analyses regression results and produces actionable verification reports.

STM32F407-driven solid state power controller channel with isolated gate driver, Kelvin-shunt current sensing and dual-path (I²t + DESAT) overcurrent protection. The single-channel design scales to a 16-channel, 28 V, 25 A/channel module.
